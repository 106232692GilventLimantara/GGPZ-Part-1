About Our Company

Founded in 2026, GGPZ (Global Grid Programming Zone) Cyber Solutions partners with businesses, schools, and government agencies to protect critical systems and data from evolving cyber threats. Our team designs secure digital infrastructure, runs incident reporting platforms, and delivers cyber awareness programs that turn everyday staff into a company's first line of defence.

As we expand our digital team, we're looking for people who care about doing security work that actually protects people — not just ticking compliance boxes.
